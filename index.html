Baby Turtle Simulator

Crawl from the nest, across the beach, and through the gate into the sea.

The left stick moves. Up follows the camera, and left or right move sideways. Only the right stick turns the camera, including up and down. Space or B brakes. Collect a spinning question-mark item box; X uses its ability. E or Y uses your turtle's own move: Snappy snaps, Leatherback body slams, Kempy spin dashes (Regular has none, but holds 2 power-ups).

Pick a turtle:

A second hatchling follows behind you. Sand Spray only catches a racer in the cloud behind your shell. The pale band at the shoreline is the wave zone.

Rocks and logs block the crawl, so steer around them. The crosshair shows where you are aiming. Left click, F, or RT shoots a bubble along the camera. A hit traps another turtle or a seagull for a short time.

Watch for the seagull near the middle of the dry sand. It chases at a little under your speed, and a hit knocks you both back. If your health reaches zero it carries you back toward the nest.

In a local race, people beside the track throw nets. Dodge the glowing landing circle: a net slows you for 2.6 seconds without taking health. Shell Guard or Kempy's spin can block a hit.

Start race stays on this computer. An online room shares the countdown, item boxes and finish order. The server decides places.

On one computer, set one window to Keyboard only and the other to Controller only.
